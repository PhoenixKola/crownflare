# Project Crownflare — Game Vision & Creative Bible

**Source of truth:** master handoff rev 1.0. This is an operational art/creative brief, not a second authority. All proper nouns are provisional and must undergo release-name clearance later.

## The promise

A spectacular premium pixel-art martial fighter crosses broken suspended arenas of the **Lumen Spindle**, an orbital machine that once stabilized day and night. Its fragments are living arenas, shattered elemental laws animate guardians and bosses, and each expedition gathers volatile **Resonance** to awaken dangerous transformations. Tone: exhilarating, enigmatic, colorful, characterful, never relentlessly bleak. Restrained environmental story, occasional exceptional dialogue and evolving hub beats. Fast, readable, authored combat; meaningful room-choice runs; transformation should feel like a fundamental new body, not a damage color filter.

## Protagonist — one design to pursue

**Iona Rell, The Courier (recommended locked *direction*, pending owner art lock):** adult kinetic duelist and former Spindle courier. Compact martial posture, athletic and readable; pearl-silver asymmetrically clipped hair with a single teal lock; dark navy cropped expedition jacket; subtle copper wrist hardware; pale sash/ribbon; practical compact boots. Head/shoulder/hand shapes must work in **48–72 logical-pixel** test scales, not just enlarged concept portraits. No purple-haired, bronze-cuff older-project look; no familiar protected franchise shapes. Her throughline is **split-ring orbit + resonance marks + pressure without combustion**. Name and accent quantities remain editable before B01 owner lock. Left-facing asymmetry needs intentional corrections, not blind mirroring.

## Form identity matrix

| Identity | Silhouette when VFX disabled | Motion / combat | Aura / color / audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base — Courier | Human scale, clean head/torso/arms/feet, one trailing sash | Light 3-hit martial chain, launcher, agile grounded footwork, dodge, small energy pressure option | Navy, pearl, teal, copper; crisp body impact, light textile movement |
| Dawnrush — First Radiance | Hair/shadow pushes upright; forearm/shoulder pressure bands; scarf spirals; minimal floating seams | Faster grounded dash, committed heat-pressure stacks, ripping pressure lance and stack-spending finisher | Amber-to-coral filaments, drawn-in dust, pulsing pressure loop; warm ignition and punch bloom |
| Stormhalo — Resonant Crown | Split hovering halo, long collar/coat fins, angular arms, subtly altered iris | Precision phase-step, counter/reversal, poised stops, targeted bursts | Indigo/white-violet prismatic electric geometry; crystalline snap and spacious decay |
| Eclipse Warden — Final Metamorphosis | **New head/mask/floating crown; tall lean hover; plated segmented limbs; mantle instead of sash; detached orbit blades** | New hover/jump, original combo/air moves, staged time-pressure ultimate with silence → charge → release | Near-black/ivory edges, coral singularity center, living dual aura, synchronized environment reaction; powerful but accessible |

All four must be distinguishable as single-color silhouettes at actual gameplay scale. Form III deserves its own canonical sprite masters, individual body-action architecture and action/effect/audio catalog. It must retain tiny signs of Iona's identity while unmistakably becoming an otherworldly form.

## Visual target / art-lock procedure

Premium **authored side-view pixel art** with strong silhouette and economical curated shading, expressive anticipation/contact/recoil/in-between sequences, parallax environments, original effects aligned to a sharp pixel grid. Preliminary logical viewport **480×270 (16:9)**; compare hero heights **48 / 60 / 72 px** in real play. These are experiments, not irrevocable numbers. HD illustration is reserved for portraits/menus/cinematics, never a replacement for sprites.

**B00 provisional board:** `art-source/characters/iona/b00-provisional-visual-target-board.png` gives one coherent original concept direction, all four form ideas, palettes and an environment mock. Its illustrations and silhouette thumbnails are not measured 48/60/72-logical-pixel gameplay studies, are not runtime pixel sprites and are not approved hero art. At B01, make four black silhouettes at identical 48/60/72-pixel heights on the same ground line, with VFX off, and show them inside the actual chamber. Generate at most 1–3 refined base masters after that review and request explicit owner signoff for one immutable reference. Every new frame must derive from the approved master; never roll a fresh unrelated character for each pose.

## Story architecture (first-release plan)

Act I: expedition into destabilized courier districts; old delivery routes become combat corridors. Act II: Spindle debris alters gravity and natural laws, strange guardians reveal the cost of Resonance. Final ascent: the Crown becomes Eclipse Warden or Iona must reject its cost, ending resolves the first game's arc. Dialogue should be short and emotionally specific; no exposition walls. Route choice can show distinct lore without sprawling quests.

## Inspiration vs originality

Dead Cells → feel of tight traversal and timing; Astral Ascent → rich pixel arenas and choices; Skul → silhouette-specific movesets; anime escalation → power anticipation and emotional rupture. Do **not** borrow character hair, costumes, dialogue, energy signature, attack poses or protected designs. Avoid literal copied aura geometry and famous move nomenclature. The Spindle's orbital-pressure vocabulary is the design source.
