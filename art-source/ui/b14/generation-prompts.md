## Title background

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: full-bleed title-screen background for an original premium pixel-art side-scrolling action roguelite
Primary request: create a beautiful, cinematic opening image for Crownflare, an ancient world whose broken daylight is being restored through the Lumen Spindle.
Scene/backdrop: a vast ruined observatory and fractured bridge suspended above dark clouds; enormous interlocking spindle rings and delicate brass mechanisms rise into a dramatic split dawn sky. Distant floating architecture creates depth and scale.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape game title art. Keep the left 42 percent substantially darker, quieter and lower contrast for a large title and two menu buttons. Place the most detailed luminous architecture and glowing spindle structure across the right half and upper distance. Leave clear foreground floor along the lower right for the existing playable hero sprite to be overlaid in code.
Style/medium: richly detailed, hand-authored 16-bit pixel art background, crisp intentional pixel clusters, sophisticated layered parallax feel, premium original indie game key art, restrained atmospheric dithering, no vector or smooth painterly gradients.
Lighting/mood: hopeful but mysterious; a warm copper and apricot dawn breaks through cool deep navy and teal night, the spindle emits a small clean cyan-gold glow.
Color palette: ink navy, desaturated indigo, dark teal, warm copper, pale apricot and restrained luminous mint.
Constraints: environment only, no character, no people, no text, no typography, no logo, no interface, no watermark. The dark left-side negative space must remain readable under light-colored menu text. Avoid repetitive circular UI rings or an empty abstract gradient.
