The completion exporter applies cell extraction, generated-fringe alpha thresholding, nearest-neighbor scaling and foot-pivot placement only. It records each runtime frame's source sheet, source cell, facing provenance, geometry and hash in `art-source/manifests/dawnrush-b05-v1.json`. Twenty-four left-facing runtime frames have true generated sources; other left entries remain explicitly identified mechanical mirrors. The retained sheets are the masters; the exporter does not synthesize character anatomy.
